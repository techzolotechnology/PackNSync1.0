# ILOMAP / MapsGPT

## One Line
ILOMAP is a map you can talk to for personalized places, group plans, saved social spots, and multi-day trips.

## Core Promise
Users ask in natural language, like:
- "romantic drinks, dim lights, quiet enough to talk"
- "where do locals actually eat in DC Chinatown"
- "plan 3 days in Tokyo with coffee, vintage shops, and no rushed mornings"

The app returns real places, explains why they fit, shows them on a map, and turns them into shareable plans.

## Trip Planner Inspiration: Wonderplan
Wonderplan is an AI-powered trip planner concept focused on making travel planning simple from start to finish. ILOMAP should borrow this strength: personalized suggestions, curated recommendations, and budget-aware plans for solo travelers, families, and friend groups.

Use this idea to shape the trip planner:
- ask for interests, preferences, budget, dates, pace, and travel style
- recommend places and activities that match the traveler type
- build an easy day-by-day plan, not a generic list
- reduce planning stress with clear choices and simple edits

## Target Users
- Friends planning nights out or trips
- Travelers who hate generic lists
- Solo backpackers, families, and friend groups with different budgets
- People saving TikToks, screenshots, and reels but never organizing them
- Locals looking for specific vibes, not just ratings

## MVP Features
1. Chat search: user asks for places by vibe, budget, location, time, group, or intent.
2. Map results: show ranked places with short reasons and key facts.
3. AI trip planner: generate personalized day-by-day itinerary with route-aware ordering, budget fit, and traveler style.
4. Group board: friends can add, remove, comment, and vote on places.
5. Saved media import: convert TikTok links, screenshots, or text notes into mapped places.
6. Waitlist: collect email, city, travel interests, and invite source.

## Differentiator
ILOMAP uses a geospatial AI layer that understands:
- place qualities: vibe, crowd, noise, price, cuisine, lighting, safety, hours
- user intent: date night, local food, family trip, solo work session
- movement: distance, route order, day pacing, neighborhood flow
- group preference: votes, conflicts, must-haves, avoid-lists

## Success Criteria
- User can ask a place question and get useful mapped results in under 10 seconds.
- Each result has a clear "why this fits" explanation.
- A trip plan avoids impossible routing and overcrowded days.
- A group can vote and finalize a plan without leaving the app.

## Brand Voice
Friendly, useful, local, and confident. It should feel like texting a friend who knows the city, not reading a travel blog.
