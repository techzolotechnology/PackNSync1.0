# Competitor keyword analysis: group travel

**Reviewed:** 18 November 2026  
**Scope:** Group-trip planning and discovery opportunities for PickAndSync.

## What the competitor pages emphasize

The screenshot contains cropped domain labels, so it is not possible to identify every competitor from the image alone. I reviewed accessible group-travel products whose names and positioning match the visible set. This is an analysis of public page copy and features, not a paid keyword-volume report.

- **Trippal:** group trip planning, planning a trip with friends, shared preferences and budgets, choosing a destination together, and building a shared itinerary. Its message centers on helping a group agree on a plan. [Source](https://www.trippal.app/)
- **COHY:** create a travel group, plan a group trip, invite travelers, find travel companions, and join travel groups. Its page also highlights destinations, trip dates, duration, and travel type. [Source](https://www.cohy.in/create-a-group)
- **OutforMiles:** plan a trip with a group, free trip plans, day-by-day itinerary, group voting, and trip chat. It also promotes bus and hotel booking; PickAndSync should not target those booking terms unless it adds those services. [Source](https://www.outformiles.com/)
- **TripBird:** its page title positions it around a free group trip planner, itinerary, and expense splitting. The page did not expose readable content during this review, so this is limited to the page title. [Source](https://www.tripbird.in/)

These are observed keyword themes, not confirmed Google search volumes or competitor rankings.

## Work applied

## TinyFish search snapshot and applied homepage update

**Checked:** 18 November 2026 · India-targeted web and Google Play searches.

- The broad query **“trip”** returned dictionary/definition pages and Trip.com brand results. It has mixed intent and is not a realistic primary target for a group-planning app.
- Results for **“group trip planner India”** included COHY, WanderOn and dedicated group-planner apps such as Triplly and Chuni. Their snippets repeatedly lead with planning a trip with friends, shared itineraries, group coordination and expenses.
- Results for **“plan a trip with friends”** included a dedicated collaborative trip-planning landing page. This supports targeting the phrase through PickAndSync's actual shared-trip workflow.
- The customer app's public Play listing is titled **“PickAndSync: Trips & Rentals”**; its short description already mentions group trips. The public long description exposes an internal “Search topics to use naturally” heading and keyword list. A clean draft titled **“PickAndSync: Trip Planner”** with a natural group-trip short description and feature-based long description is now saved in Play Console. The draft was not submitted, so the live listing and review status were not changed.

The homepage title, description and visible heading now lead with **group trip planner / plan group trips with friends**, while retaining the real itinerary, expense-splitting and vehicle-rental value. The existing `/trips/` page remains the focused destination for group-trip searches.

Examples observed in results: [COHY group creation](https://cohy.in/create-a-group), [WanderOn](https://wanderon.in/), [Triplly group trip planner](https://triplly.app/group-trip-planner), [Chuni on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=app.ctp.planner), and [SmartTrippy's collaborative planner](https://smarttrippy.com/plan-trip-with-friends).
