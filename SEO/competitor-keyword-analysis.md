# Competitor keyword analysis: group travel

**Reviewed:** 18 November 2026  
**Scope:** Group-trip planning and discovery opportunities for PickAndSync.

## What the competitor pages emphasize

The screenshot contains cropped domain labels, so it is not possible to identify every competitor from the image alone. I reviewed accessible group-travel products whose names and positioning match the visible set. This is an analysis of public page copy and features, not a paid keyword-volume report.

- **Trippal:** group trip planning, planning a trip with friends, shared preferences and budgets, choosing a destination together, and building a shared itinerary. Its message centers on helping a group agree on a plan. [Source](https://www.trippal.app/)
- **COHY:** create a travel group, plan a group trip, invite travelers, find travel companions, and join travel groups. Its page also highlights destinations, trip dates, duration, and travel type. [Source](https://www.cohy.in/create-a-group)
- **OutforMiles:** plan a trip with a group, free trip plans, day-by-day itinerary, group voting, and trip chat. It also promotes bus and hotel booking; PickAndSync should not target those booking terms unless it adds those services. [Source](https://www.outformiles.com/)
- **TripBird:** its page title positions it around a free group trip planner, itinerary, and expense splitting. The page did not expose readable content during this review, so this is limited to the page title. [Source](https://www.tripbird.in/)

These are observed keyword themes, not confirmed Google search volumes or competitor rankings.

## Work applied

- Updated the visible Trips page heading and introduction around **plan a group trip with friends**.
- Updated the server-delivered `/trips/` fallback content with crawlable headings, concise feature copy, and links to trip creation, the India group-planning checklist, and the cost splitter.
- Avoided unsupported promises such as verified travel companions, hotel or bus booking, AI-generated itineraries, and guaranteed savings.
