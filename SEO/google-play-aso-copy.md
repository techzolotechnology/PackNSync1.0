# Google Play listing copy: PickAndSync apps

**Prepared:** 18 November 2026
**Purpose:** Search-friendly, accurate English listing copy for the customer and host apps.

Google Play currently allows up to 30 characters for an app name, 80 for the short description, and 4,000 for the full description. The copy below stays within those limits and follows Play's guidance to keep metadata accurate and avoid repetitive keyword lists. [Listing setup and limits](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9859152) · [Store listing best practices](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/13393723)

## Customer app

**Package:** `com.packandsync.app`

**App name — 28/30 characters**

> PickAndSync: Trips & Rentals

**Short description — 66/80 characters**

> Plan group trips, split costs, and book self-drive cars and bikes.

**Full description**

Plan trips with friends, keep shared costs clear, and find self-drive cars and bikes in one app. PickAndSync helps travelers organize a group trip and manage rental requests in India.

PLAN A GROUP TRIP

Discover open group trips or create a trip with your destination, dates and estimated budget. Invite friends, share trip details, and keep conversations and plans connected to the trip.

SPLIT SHARED TRIP COSTS

Record shared expenses with your group and review who paid and each person’s balance. Use the trip cost splitter to estimate a fair share and make settling up easier.

FIND A SELF-DRIVE RENTAL

Search cars and bikes by location and travel dates. Review listing details, send a booking request, and follow its status in the app. Available vehicles and dates depend on local host listings.

MANAGE BOOKINGS AND WALLET

Keep track of rental requests, confirmed booking details, payment steps and your wallet balance. Identity checks and rental terms may be required before booking.

PickAndSync brings group trip planning, trip expense splitting and local self-drive rentals together, so your group can keep the plan and travel details in sync.

## Host app

**Package:** `com.pickandsync.host`

**App name — 25/30 characters**

> PickAndSync Host: Rentals

**Short description — 73/80 characters**

> List cars, bikes or scooters, manage bookings, availability and earnings.

**Full description**

PickAndSync Host helps vehicle owners and rental businesses publish eligible vehicles and manage rental activity from one app.

LIST YOUR VEHICLES

Create a vehicle listing with its category, model, location and rental details. Set up an individual host or rental shop profile, then follow the steps shown for review and publishing.

MANAGE BOOKING REQUESTS

Review incoming requests, check booking details, and use the available host actions to respond. Keep track of upcoming and active rentals from the host dashboard.

CONTROL FLEET AND AVAILABILITY

View your vehicle list and calendar to manage availability around bookings. Check booking summaries and earnings information in the app.

COMPLETE VERIFICATION STEPS

Submit the identity and vehicle documents requested for your account or listing. Some listings require verification and approval before they can be published.

PickAndSync Host is for vehicle owners and rental businesses offering eligible vehicles through PickAndSync. Listing availability, booking volume and earnings depend on location, approval and customer demand.

## Keyword coverage

- **Customer app:** group trip planner, plan trips with friends, split trip costs, trip expense splitter, self-drive car rental, bike rental.
- **Host app:** list a car for rent, list a bike for rent, scooter rental host, manage rental bookings, vehicle host app.
- Phrases appear in normal descriptions instead of repeated keyword blocks. The customer description does not promise hotel or bus booking, guaranteed availability, or automatic trip approval. The host description does not promote helicopter hosting while its live inquiry flow is unresolved.

## Applying the copy

In Play Console, open each app’s **Grow users → Store presence → Main store listing**, replace the app name and descriptions, review the preview, then save and submit the listing changes. Store listing text is shared across testing tracks, so testers may see the update before a production release. Publishing the metadata does not require a new app bundle.
